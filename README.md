## MSME Credit Access & Regional Formalization (India) 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

An empirical policy and data analysis study evaluating digital MSME formalization (Udyam & UAP) against formal RBI Priority Sector Lending across all 36 Indian States and Union Territories (10.22 Cr MSMEs; Rs. 34.43 Lakh Cr bank credit).

## 🔍 Key Analytical Insights

* * Regional Credit Gap: High-registration states like UP (Rs. 2.00L/unit), Bihar (Rs. 1.12L), and West Bengal (Rs. 2.45L) trail Gujarat (Rs. 5.23L) and Maharashtra (Rs. 4.28L) in per-enterprise credit deployment.

* * Scaling Bottleneck: Over 98% of Udyam units remain Micro; states with higher Small/Medium graduation rates (Delhi 2.77%, Gujarat 2.05%) secure larger average credit tickets.

 * *Women-Owned MSMEs: Manipur (34.2%), Mizoram (33.5%), and Kerala (27.3%) lead the 23.93% state average.
